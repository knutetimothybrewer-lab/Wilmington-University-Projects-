# Instructional coverage

Every chapter has a definition, three substantive analysis paragraphs, educational challenge/leadership implications, elementary and secondary applications, disability and multilingual/access considerations, limitations, three actionable strategies, an activity, a reflection, and synthesis. Presenter and study modes share the same content; study opens the analysis/examples by default.

## Cross-cutting implementation

- Three narrative acts: establish direction → build conditions → lead with evidence.
- Original SVG classroom opens/closes the journey. Chapter environments shift displays/furniture to reflect planning, governance, assessment, evaluation, and inclusive learning. Conceptual diagrams have equivalent text.
- Natural scroll, restrained camera movement, sticky figure placement, progressive reveals, animated organizational links, conceptual timelines, and state transitions. No continuous particle animation or WebGL requirement.
- Local reflections and activity state, chapter completion, reset, sharable chapter hashes, fullscreen fallback, chapter navigation, keyboard controls, and motion preference.
- Source-specific coverage is partial where full readings are unavailable; the app states that limitation rather than substituting invented attribution.
