CLOSING · 3 MINUTES

The tools change. Human responsibility remains.

AI literacy is not simply knowing how to use artificial intelligence. It is understanding how AI works, critically evaluating its outputs, recognizing its limitations, and exercising responsible human judgment.

Reveal the five-chapter synthesis
  1. Understand overlapping mechanisms and tasks.
  2. Distinguish generation, context, and evidence.
  3. Trace claims to sources and verify support.
  4. Investigate recommendations before acting.
  5. Use collaboration to strengthen learning and agency.
